🎓 Academic Explanation & Foundational Math

1. The Stochastic Markov Decision Process (MDP)

This smart grid optimization task is modeled as a continuous-state, continuous-action stochastic Markov Decision Process (MDP) defined by the five-tuple \((\mathcal{S}, \mathcal{A}, P, R, \gamma)\). The continuous state space \(\mathcal{S}\) tracks four dimensions: the battery normalized State of Charge (SoC), electricity pricing, solar irradiance generation, and the time of day. Crucially, the transition dynamics function \(P(s' | s, a)\) incorporates environmental volatility through first-order Autoregressive (AR(1)) stochastic processes mapping both the pricing schedule and local photovoltaic generation:

\[p_{t+1} = \mu_p + \phi_p(p_t - \mu_p) + \epsilon^p_t, \quad \epsilon^p_t \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_p^2)\] \[g_{t+1} = \mu_g + \phi_g(g_t - \mu_g) + \epsilon^g_t, \quad \epsilon^g_t \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_g^2)\]

Here, the stochastic price and solar signals are updated using stationary coefficients \(\phi_p = 0.8\) and \(\phi_g = 0.7\), combined with independent normal error perturbations \(\epsilon^p\) and \(\epsilon^g\) that prevent unrealistic high-frequency variance while retaining daily periodicity constraints.

2. Soft Actor-Critic (SAC) Maximum Entropy Objective

To operate effectively in highly volatile and unpredictable pricing landscapes, the decision agent is trained using the off-policy Soft Actor-Critic (SAC) framework. SAC expands the traditional expected return formulation of reinforcement learning by maximizing the policy's structural entropy alongside standard long-term reward. The maximum entropy objective function \(J(\pi)\) is mathematically stated as:

\[J(\pi) = \sum_{t=0}^{\infty} \mathbb{E}_{(s_t, a_t) \sim \rho_\pi} \left[ R(s_t, a_t) + \alpha \mathcal{H}(\pi(\cdot | s_t)) \right]\]

where \(\mathcal{H}(\pi(\cdot | s_t)) = -\int_{A} \pi(a|s_t) \log \pi(a|s_t) da\) is the operational policy entropy, and \(\alpha\) is the dynamic temperature parameter balancing exploration drive against standard optimization. By incentivizing the neural network to explore highly diverse path sequences, entropy regularization prevents premature collapse of the continuous control actor into sub-optimal local basins (such as simple, short-sighted greedy trading schemes).

3. Physics Boundary Constraints & Reward Shaped Calculus

To enforce grid stability, system safety, and longevity, the reward framework employs multi-objective reward shaping. The aggregate reward \(R(s,a)\) is defined as:

\[R(s, a) = R_{\text{arbitrage}} + R_{\text{green}} - R_{\text{wear}}\]

Where energy arbitrage \(R_{\text{arbitrage}} = p_t \times (D_t - C_t) \times \eta\) optimizes direct grid cost-efficiency, utilizing local charging efficiency \(\eta\) that degrades quadratically as SoC approaches full capacity: \(\eta_{\text{charge}} = \eta_{\text{base}} \times (1 - 0.2 \cdot SoC^2)\). Degradation penalty \(R_{\text{wear}}\) implements dynamic Depth-of-Discharge (DoD) wear tracking proportional to absolute power flow, incentivizing battery longevity. This shaped manifold maps a highly constrained safe operational frontier directly within the continuous neural network optimization landscape.
